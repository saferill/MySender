# Privacy Policy for MySender

Last updated: September 2026

MySender is an open-source, peer-to-peer local file sharing application. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
MySender **does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data**. The application operates entirely locally on your devices. There are no tracking scripts, telemetries, or remote analytics of any kind.

## 2. Local Network Operation Only
All device discovery and file transfers occur directly over your Local Area Network (Wi-Fi or Hotspot) using peer-to-peer communication. No files, metadata, or communications are routed through external or cloud servers.

## 3. End-to-End Encryption
All communication and data transfer between devices are encrypted by default using TLS (Transport Layer Security) with certificates generated on-the-fly directly on your device.

## 4. Permissions
Any device permissions requested by MySender (such as Storage, Photos, and Local Network Access) are strictly used to read files you choose to send and save files you receive on your local device.

## 5. No Third-Party Ads
MySender is 100% free and contains no advertisements, trackers, or commercial monetization mechanisms.

## 6. Open Source
The complete source code of MySender is transparent and publicly auditable on GitHub:
https://github.com/saferill/MySender
